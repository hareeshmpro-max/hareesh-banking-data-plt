# Business Rules

1. Only SUCCESS transactions are included in financial analytics.
2. Transaction amount must be greater than zero.
3. Transaction account_id must exist in the account master.
4. Account customer_id must exist in customer master.
5. Loan amount must be greater than zero.
6. Loan status must be ACTIVE or CLOSED.
7. Duplicate business keys are invalid source records.
8. Monthly metrics use calendar months.
